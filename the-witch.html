Project

THE WITCH

This is one of my favourite projects I've created to date. The Witch is a short story written by Efraim Sidon, which I turned into a 32-page illustrated book using acrylic paint on paper, with a touch of fineliner.

I loved creating the quirky characters and using the painting style to give the book a nostalgic, handmade feel.

Challenges

  • Since the story has so little text, I had to think of creative ways to fill the pages with illustrations that added something new to the story. I wanted to create a story within a story, rather than simply illustrate what was already written.
  • The painting technique was quite time-consuming, especially with such a short deadline. It meant a lot of patience and many late nights!
  • The printing stage is always a challenge. I had to make sure the colours came out as close to the originals as possible, while also choosing the right paper type and weight. It was my first time printing a larger project as a hardcover book at a professional printing house, so I had to learn how the whole process worked and deal with all the technical details and back-and-forth involved.
