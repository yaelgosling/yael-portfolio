Project

MTV IDENT

This project is an MTV ident I created to mark the channel's closing. I decided to explore the weird world of technology, babies, and surreal situations, combining two different mediums to make the whole thing feel even more strange and unexpected.

Challenges

  • Finding a way to create a cohesive video using two different styles. It was my first time combining realistic pencil drawings with bold, colourful digital illustrations.
  • Figuring out how to tell a clear story in just 10 seconds, without using any words.
